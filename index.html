Our Policies & Guarantees

Transparent, fair, and designed to protect your special moments.

🎨 Customization Policy

Every web project includes personalized names, dates, custom photos, audio tracks, and custom love letters. You provide your requirements during or after order submission.

⚡ Delivery Policy

Standard delivery is within 24 to 48 hours. Once completed, you receive a private staging URL to preview and test before sharing with your partner.

🔄 Revision Policy

We include unlimited text and typo revisions, along with up to 2 design layout adjustments to ensure your project looks absolutely perfect.

🤝 Cash on Delivery & Pay-After-Preview Policy

Enjoy complete peace of mind with our transparent pay-after-preview policy. You only pay after you receive, review, and approve your customized digital preview, ensuring you are 100% satisfied with every detail before finalizing your payment.

🛠️ After-Sales Support & Free Web Setup

We provide complete web setup and comprehensive post-sale support free of cost. If you experience any technical difficulties, link issues, or need help launching your romantic website, our support team is available via Instagram DM and Facebook Messenger to fix it for you immediately.

🔒 Privacy & Data Security Policy

Your photos, love letters, and personal moments are 100% private. We never sell, share, or publicly display your projects without your explicit permission. Password-protected links are available upon request.
